## Question

Update the accepted product and garment-catalog specifications so they consistently encode the confirmed wardrobe-first scope, GPT-proposed colors, Low-quality 816 × 816 defaults, native navigation, Source Photo detail visibility, Shelf Image versioning, lightweight accounts, NAS deployment, offline boundary, and backup requirement.

## Resolution

The accepted product and Shelf Image specifications now make the persistent wardrobe the first usable release. They define GPT-proposed editable colors, Low-quality 816 × 816 defaults, native Owning/Wanting navigation with toolbar Add and zoomable detail media, durable Source Photo provenance, immutable/restorable Shelf Image Versions, administrator-created accounts, NAS Docker Compose behind Tailscale HTTPS, the explicit offline boundary, and a backup/restore drill. Fitting profiles and Looks move to a separate later effort.
