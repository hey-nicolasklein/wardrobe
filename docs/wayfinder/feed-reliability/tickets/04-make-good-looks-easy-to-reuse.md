## What

Add durable favorites for Looks and let a person filter the Feed by a wardrobe item. Keep the existing generation, variation, details, download, and deletion actions.

Occasion input is also absent from the composer. Defer it until the product defines supported occasions and how they should affect planning. Do not add a free-text occasion field as part of this ticket.

## Why

The Feed becomes less useful as it grows when a good outfit can only be found by scrolling. Favorites and item filtering answer the immediate reuse need with clear behavior. Occasion input needs a separate product decision because it changes how the planner selects an outfit and scene.
