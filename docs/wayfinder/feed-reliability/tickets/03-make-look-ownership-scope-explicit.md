## What

Default the Look composer to owned clothing and show a clear, explicit control for including wanted clothing. Label any wanted items that remain selectable. Apply the chosen scope consistently to exact-item and category constraints.

The current service intentionally uses owned items for an unconstrained surprise Look, then includes both owned and wanted items for a deliberate request. The composer lists eligible items without showing their ownership. Category constraints therefore may select wanted pieces without making that result clear.

## Why

People need to know whether a Look contains clothes they can wear now or clothes they are considering. The current behavior supports virtual try-ons, but the hidden scope makes an ordinary category request ambiguous.
