Fit photos use your selected photos directly, without a generated identity sheet.

1. Reference photos

Photos from Downloads. Choose up to eight for a photo board, or up to four for a sheet. The selected order follows the gallery. Refinement reserves the first reference for its parent sheet.

Use Crop to keep only the person or area you want. The original file stays unchanged.

Converted JPEG or PNG copies appear in place of matching HEIC files.

2. Choose clothing

Copied from your running wardrobe. Choose up to three items. The photo board counts as one image, leaving room for three clothing images. Separate-photo mode shares four inputs across photos and clothing.

3. Generation

A board combines up to eight cropped photos without redrawing them. It counts as one input, leaving room for three clothing images. The top-left photo is the primary reference.

Changing selections or scene reloads the default prompt. Edit it afterward to test a variant. Fit photos use the app's provider at medium quality, 1024 × 1280. Sheets use high quality, 864 × 1536.

Each run makes a paid image request through the configured worker provider. Files and reviews stay in .prototype-data/likeness-lab.

4. Compare and diagnose

The first selected identity photo sets the face angle and expression. Crop away other people before generating. Clothing images follow the identity photos in the request.

Review facial likeness and clothing accuracy separately. Copy a run's settings to keep the inputs fixed while testing a different prompt.
